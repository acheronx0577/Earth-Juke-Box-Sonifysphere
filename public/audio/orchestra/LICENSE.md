# Orchestra samples

These files are individual notes from the FluidR3_GM soundfont, as published by
the midi-js-soundfonts project:

https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts

FluidR3_GM was released by Frank Wen under the MIT License. The notes used
here are `cello`, `contrabass`, `string_ensemble_1`, `timpani`, and
`orchestral_harp`. Together they are about 3.4 MB and are fetched only after
someone chooses Orchestra.
