Which layout makes the sound easy to explore?

Three practical directions for one experience: upload an image or video, move over the media to hear its pattern, inspect the live result, then choose an instrument and save a region sound profile. Each direction is shown on desktop and phone.

Shared rule: show one image or one normal video player. Video analysis happens behind the scenes; no visible frame strip. Audio starts after the person taps Play sound. NASA and marine readings appear only when the source, place, and time support them.

Why it is easy to use

Upload, exploration, and sound choice have a visible order. A first-time person sees one task at a time while the result stays directly beside or below the media.

Where it gives up space

The step panel consumes desktop width. On phone, the result and sound controls need scrolling after the media.

Patterns inspected: fal input/result layout, Hume AI setup and analysis, and Alan phone preview with simple completion controls. These are layout precedents; the content and workflow shown here are proposed for SonifySphere.

Why it is easy to use

The image or video is the obvious interaction target. The live reading stays close to it, and the sound button is visible without opening another screen.

Where it needs guidance

A new person may not know to drag or hover. Use a one-time “Drag to hear” hint and a sample sound button.

Patterns inspected: Shopify media canvas with right tool panel, Grok spacious media preview, and Instagram phone image with bottom controls. We borrow the arrangement, not the editing tools.

Why it is easy to use

People can compare sounds with clear names and preview controls. A saved profile records the source, region, instrument, variant, and data provenance.

Where it gets busy

Four sound rows can distract from reading the media. On phone, keep two choices visible and put the rest behind “More sounds.”

Patterns inspected: ElevenLabs composition with variations, Suno generated sound list, and TikTok phone sound picker. Their timeline and editing features are deliberately absent here.
